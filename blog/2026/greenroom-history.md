At the bar after a Space Sailer race event hosted by EFYC a guy came up to me and said "I built your boat". It was Brian Saleeba from SoPYC, who sails a SS27 now. He'd seen me on the river and recognised the old fading paint job. I grabbed his contact and he ended up emailing me lots of interesting details and photos.

> Brett,
>
> That was good to catch up with you at EFYC last Sun.
> Your yacht has a thoroughbred history as it became the top performing SS22 in its day.
> The pics attached show the beast on my front lawn where I fitted it out.
> Another one is launching day at SPYC in Oct ’77.
> The other one shows that she was no stranger to the ocean.
> I will send some more pics on another email.
>
>Regards,
>
>Brian

> Brett,
>
> So far so good then.
> These pics show the result of another successful season (including my daughter who skippered it in the ladies’ skippers’ race).
> In Geordie Bay with the crew.
> Somewhere on the Swan.
> I had the beast for 12 years and during that time she won many club and State championship series.
> In 1989 I had my new boat, the SS27 also in the front lawn while I fitted it out.
> One day a cousin and his wife visited and as they left, she said that she would like to purchase the 22.
> I said that was OK but she couldn’t have the name. So she settled on Leda from the mythological Leda and the Swan.
> She sailed it out of CYC with a group of ladies as the crew.
> Unfortunately she came down with some health problems and sold the boat.
> Over the years I noticed it languishing on a mooring in Matilda Bay in a non-cared for condition, which was a great shame for such a pedigreed vessel.
> Hopefully you can get some of the satisfaction that I enjoyed while I had it.
>
> Regards,
>
> Brian
