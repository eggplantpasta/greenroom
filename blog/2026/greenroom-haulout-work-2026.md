> **Deadline rule:** 23 December is a hard stop. Finish sound laminate repairs and safe, protected underwater surfaces before attempting cosmetic perfection. If the boat cannot be made seaworthy in time, organise a yard-approved alternative departure rather than force an unsafe launch.

## Work scope and order

### Priority 1 - make the hull safe

- [ ] Lift and wash; inspect for osmosis, keel pitting and damage.
- [ ] Remove and structurally glass over **four** old openings: **25 mm sink**, **two 25 mm cockpit drains**, **10 mm anchor well**.
- [ ] Drill and install **three new 25 mm TruDesign outlets**: two cockpit drains through the hull sides, one anchor-well drain well above the waterline.
- [ ] Verify the marked outlet positions are above the applicable **heeled waterline**, consistent with the fitting manufacturer's no-valve installation requirements; check hose fall and clear bore.
- [ ] Seal exposed new-hole laminate/core, fit hose and clamps, support hoses and water-test drains.
- [ ] Repair limited localised laminate damage or osmosis **only if drying, layup and full immersion cure can be completed**.
- [ ] Investigate any serious keel/joint defects rather than hiding them under filler or primer.

### Priority 2 - durable protection

- [ ] Remove/abrade exhausted antifoul and failed underwater coatings using yard-approved containment; preserve sound underlying gelcoat/laminate.
- [ ] Prepare and roughly fair the underwater hull and four repair locations.
- [ ] Remove loose keel coating, treat corrosion/pitting and promptly prime any bare substrate with the correct metal-compatible product.
- [ ] Apply manufacturer-specified underwater epoxy primer/barrier system if a complete, immersion-ready coating programme fits the time available.
- [ ] Apply antifouling within the primer's specified recoat/tie-coat and launch windows.

### Priority 3 - appearance

- [ ] Sand back aged topsides coatings to a sound, compatible substrate.
- [ ] Fill and fair obvious damage, without chasing a perfect racing finish.
- [ ] Prime/undercoat topsides as required and roll the **G32 Opaline** finish.
- [ ] Tidy waterline and details where time and cure permit.

## Work instructions and quality checks

### 1. Lift, wash and inspect

- [ ] Pressure wash before growth dries; mark waterline and photograph the hull.
- [ ] Inspect for local blisters, wet laminate, cracks, loose old paint, keel corrosion and joint movement.
- [ ] Separate cosmetic defects from safety-critical damage; add findings to the defect register.
- [ ] Avoid beginning extensive osmosis excavation without a credible drying and repair schedule.

### 2. Close four old through-hulls

- [ ] Confirm and label: sink 25 mm, port cockpit 25 mm, starboard cockpit 25 mm, anchor-well 10 mm.
- [ ] Remove all old metal, obsolete hose and fittings.
- [ ] Check laminate thickness, condition and access on both sides.
- [ ] Prepare appropriate wide scarf/taper to sound glass for each hole (often about 12:1, subject to the actual laminate repair specification).
- [ ] Laminate with a suitable glass/epoxy repair schedule, properly wetted, avoiding a filler-only plug.
- [ ] Allow specified cure, check for voids/soft laminate, wash off blush if relevant, then fair/seal and photograph each repair.
- [ ] Confirm no leaks, pinholes, exposed fibres or incomplete immersion protection before launch.

### 3. Fit three new 25 mm drains

- [ ] Confirm the outlet coordinates relative to upright and heeled waterline **before cutting**.
- [ ] Confirm the exact TruDesign models and the manufacturer's permitted no-valve installation at these heights.
- [ ] Confirm two 25 mm cockpit drains satisfy the applicable class/use rule and retain its reference; ensure true hose bore and usable flow are not restricted.
- [ ] Dry-fit short, falling, serviceable hose runs through the isolated aft locker; check chafe and backflow.
- [ ] Cut holes of the correct diameter, protect any core and epoxy-seal cut laminate; allow cure.
- [ ] Bed fittings using compatible sealant and the manufacturer's torque/instructions; install reinforced hose and suitable clamps/supports.
- [ ] Run water through each cockpit drain and the anchor well; inspect every connection from inside. Confirm the anchor well can clear mud/debris.
- [ ] Check flooded locker assumptions and access for emergency inspection/repair.

### 4. Prepare and roughly fair hull and keel

- [ ] Remove worn antifoul and failed layers with containment and dust control, preserving sound gelcoat.
- [ ] Remove loose/failed topsides paint and prepare a sound substrate; verify compatibility with selected primer.
- [ ] Open only localised blisters proven dryable/repairable within the launch window.
- [ ] Fill/fair repairs and imperfections conservatively; avoid chasing full cosmetic perfection.
- [ ] Inspect keel metal, remove corrosion, address pits and apply the selected substrate-specific primer promptly.
- [ ] Escalate deep corrosion, suspect keel attachment or severe laminate faults rather than filling over them.

### 5. Apply coatings

- [ ] Record exact products, numbers of coats, roller method and required film build for each substrate.
- [ ] Follow preparation, dew point, temperature, mixing, pot life, thinning and timing in each TDS/SDS.
- [ ] Keep sanding dust and grinding out of wet painting areas; protect from wind and hot hull temperatures.
- [ ] Log the time, temperature, batch, quantity and next recoat deadline for each coat.
- [ ] Respect underwater primer-to-antifoul tie-coat requirements and **minimum cure before immersion**.
- [ ] Coordinate support-pad access with yard staff; do not move support pads yourself.
- [ ] Ensure any underwater areas stripped to laminate or bare metal receive a *complete immersion-rated protective system* by launch, even if cosmetics/antifoul are postponed.

### 6. Final launch checks

- [ ] All **four** former through-hull sites structurally sound and sealed.
- [ ] All **three** new fittings sealed, secured and water-tested.
- [ ] No newly exposed underwater laminate, bare keel metal or coatings unsafe for immersion.
- [ ] No unassessed structural damage or substantial wet laminate concealed by new coatings.
- [ ] Keel and hull coating cure/launch intervals satisfied, including pad patches.
- [ ] Record remaining work in January list; photograph finished hull and repairs.
- [ ] Confirm relaunch slot, clear tools/waste and settle yard account.
- [ ] On launch, immediately inspect fitting/repair areas for leaks and confirm cockpit/anchor-well drainage and normal trim.
